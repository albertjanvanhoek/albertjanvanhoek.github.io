Who is reading this?

Stop for a moment.

Someone is reading these words. Who?

You say: “I am.” Good. Let us look for you.

AJ van Hoek · November 2026

01The parts

Look at your hand. It is made of cells, very small living things. Your body has about thirty million million of them.

Each cell has a job. Some carry air. Some fight sickness. Some carry messages. Some hold you up.

Not one of them can read. Not one of them knows your name. Not one of them knows that you are here.

And still, here you are. Reading.

02Inside

Now go inside. Look for yourself.

You find blood. You find bone. You find a brain. Look inside the brain, and you find cells again. They send small electric messages to each other. Look inside a cell, and you find parts. Inside the parts, smaller parts.

You can look for a very long time. You will not find anyone at home. There is no little you, sitting in the middle, reading.

So where are you?

03The bricks

Here is a strange fact.

Your body changes its parts. Your skin makes new skin. Your blood makes new blood. Even inside the cells that stay with you all your life, the small pieces go out and new pieces come in.

Most of the atoms in your body were not in your body a few years ago. They were in bread, in water, in air. In the sea. In a field of wheat in another country.

The child in your old photos: almost nothing of that child is still here. But you look at the photo and you say: “That is me.”

The bricks change. The house stays. How?

04Sleep

Here is another strange fact.

Last night you went to sleep. For some hours, in deep sleep without dreams, you were not there. Not happy. Not sad. Not anything.

The cells were there. They kept working. The heart. The lungs. The blood. But you stopped.

This morning you came back. Nobody called you. The cells only started to talk to each other again, in a certain way. And there you were.

You do not sit in your body like a person in a house. You happen in it.

05The hospital

Think of a hospital.

You are ill. You go in. Some days later you come out, and you are well. Who made you well?

The building? No. A building cannot do anything.

The doctor? She was with you for ten minutes. Then there was the nurse at night. The man who cleaned your room. The woman in the laboratory, whom you never saw. The people who made the medicine, in another country, years ago. The teacher who taught the doctor.

Every one of them goes home at night. Some leave for ever, and new people come. The hospital is never the same people for long.

And still the hospital makes people well. Day after day. Year after year. No one person in it can do this. The healing is not in any of them. The healing is between them.

A hospital is not a thing. It is something that happens.

06The hoover

We see this every day, and we do not see it.

Take a hoover out of its box. Plastic. Metal. Wire. Not one part can clean.

Put the parts together. Turn it on. Now there is cleaning.

Where did the cleaning come from? It was not in any part. It came from the parts, together. And we do not call this magic. We call it normal.

So here is the normal thing about you. Nature put your parts together. And now there is seeing. There is thinking. There is loving. There is reading.

Not in any part. From the parts, together.

07The answer

Now we can answer the first question.

You are not a thing that has parts. You are what the parts do, together.

You are not a noun.You are a verb.

The cells do not make you once, like a factory makes a car. They make you now. And now. And now. Every second, millions and millions of small messages go from cell to cell. That, right now, is you.

08How thin

Feel how thin this is.

Stop breathing for a few minutes, and the messages stop. And you stop. All the parts are still there. Every one of them. But the verb is gone.

A little too much heat. A little too little sugar. One small vessel closed in the wrong place.

You are not strong like a stone. You are like a song. A song is only there while someone is playing it.

09The other side

And now feel the other side.

Today your heart will beat about one hundred thousand times. You will not ask it to. It will do it.

Thirty million million cells are working for you at this moment. They do not know you. They will never know you. They do it anyway.

Every morning, out of bread and water and air, the network makes you again. Nothing had to do this. Nothing promised it.

And still it is happening. Today. Here. As you.

10A part, too

One last thing.

You are a part, too. You are a cell in a family. In a circle of friends. In a street, a city, a hospital. Some days you are the one who helps to make a stranger well.

Between you and other people, bigger things happen. Things that none of you can do alone. They live in what you send to each other: a word, a look, a kindness, a correction, a meal.

When the messages stop, those things stop too.

So be careful with what you send. Something is living in it.

Someone is reading these words.

It is not one cell. It is not one thing. It is a very old, very fragile, very beautiful kind of working together. For a short time, it answers to your name.

Look up from the page. Breathe.

It is still happening.

You are still happening.

This piece grows out of Evolution by Emergence, my work on how things keep existing through their connections.
